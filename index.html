About

A focused, hands-on partner

Beaming Cove is an independent, incorporated consultancy dedicated to the Salesforce AppExchange ecosystem. As a one-person company, you work directly with the person doing the work — no hand-offs, no layers of account management.

The focus is narrow by design: building commercial-grade Salesforce apps and guiding partners through the technical and process steps needed to publish and maintain them on AppExchange.
