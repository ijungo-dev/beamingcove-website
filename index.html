About

An independent Salesforce ISV

Beaming Cove is an independent software vendor (ISV) in the Salesforce ecosystem. We build and own our products, and we publish them on the Salesforce AppExchange under the Beaming Cove name.

Our focus is on practical, platform-native apps that are easy to install, safe to run, and maintained over the long term. We plan to offer both free and paid apps.

What we do

Our focus areas

⌘

Native Salesforce Apps

We build our apps natively on the Salesforce Platform with Apex and Lightning Web Components, so they run inside your org.

◆

Published on AppExchange

Each app is released as a managed package on the AppExchange after passing Salesforce's security review.

✓

Ongoing Maintenance & Support

We keep our apps up to date with Salesforce releases, ship improvements, and support the customers who use them.

Free and Paid Apps
